About North Star Bakery

A neighborhood bakery built on warm ovens, good ingredients, and the people who gather around them.

Our Story

North Star Bakery began as a small family kitchen with a simple goal: bake bread that makes people feel at home. Today we still bake by hand every morning, and we love seeing familiar faces at the counter.

Whether you are grabbing a loaf on your commute or ordering a cake for a family celebration, we are proud to be part of the community.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We choose simple, quality ingredients and buy from local farms and mills whenever we can. Fresh, honest ingredients make better bread, and they keep our money in the neighborhood.

Meet the Team

Head Baker

Pastry Chef
